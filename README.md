# Muhammad Qasim Hussain

I am completing an M.Sc. in Digital Engineering at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, where I conduct my Master's research in the Webis group.

My research focuses on argument identification and cross-dataset generalisation. I study how annotation guidelines, surrounding context and generated representations affect language-model predictions.

## Research

First author of:

[The Wildcards at Touché: Guideline-Conditioned Argument Identification with Dataset-Level Expert Routing](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper497.pdf)  
CLEF 2026 Working Notes.

I presented this work at CLEF 2026 in Jena and received the Touché award for the highest Supplementary score through guideline-conditioning.
